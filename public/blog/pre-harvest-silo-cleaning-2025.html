As harvest season approaches, grain facilities are gearing up for peak demand. Silos will soon be running at capacity, and even minor issues—from clogged flow to mold growth—can create costly delays. The final weeks of summer are the perfect time to make sure your storage systems are ready.

Why Cleaning Before Harvest Matters

Residue from previous cycles primes a silo for blockages and uneven flow. During harvest, those buildups can disrupt discharge, stop conveyors, and force unplanned downtime when your operation can least afford it. A pre‑harvest clean helps you:

  • Maximize storage space by removing old material and caked fines.
  • Keep grain moving with fewer hang‑ups, bridging, and rat‑holing.
  • Avoid costly downtime during the busiest weeks of the year.

Mold & Moisture: The Hidden Risk

Late‑summer heat and humidity create an ideal environment for mold. When fresh grain enters, small pockets of moisture can lead to spoilage, insect activity, or mycotoxins that threaten quality and compliance. Cleaning now removes residues that trap moisture and supports stable aeration once bins are filled.

Safety & Compliance First

Confined‑space entry remains one of OSHA’s most‑cited hazards. Silo Clean Pro technicians are licensed, insured, and trained in OSHA‑approved procedures, including lockout/tagout, atmospheric testing, ventilation, and rescue readiness—helping you protect people and maintain compliance.

Pre‑Harvest Action Plan

  1. Empty and isolate bins; lockout/tagout and ventilate.
  2. Dry clean walls, ledges, roof, and reclaim points; vacuum fines.
  3. Inspect for leaks, corrosion, coating damage, and loose fasteners.
  4. Sanitize where appropriate; follow label for concentration and contact time.
  5. Verify aeration performance and dry‑out before closing up.
  6. Document findings and schedule follow‑ups during harvest.

Act Before Harvest Begins

Once silos are full, cleaning options are limited and downtime is far more expensive. Taking proactive steps now is the most effective way to protect your operation and ensure a smooth harvest.
