Routine inspections keep silos safe, efficient, and compliant. Beyond a quick walk‑around, a good inspection follows a consistent checklist and documents what you see. Here’s a field‑tested guide to seven checkpoints—and what to look for at each stop.

1) Roof and Vents

Check roof sheets, penetrations, and vents for damage, blocked airflow, and corrosion.

2) Wall Seams and Fasteners

Walk seams panel‑by‑panel; look for streaking, loose/broken hardware, and gasket failure.

3) Foundation and Base Ring

Note settlement, cracked grout, standing water, and anchor fastener condition.

4) Ladders, Cages, and Platforms

Verify rungs, cages, rails, gates, and anchors meet safe access requirements.

5) Moisture Ingress and Corrosion

Track staining to its source; address coating damage and underfilm corrosion early.

6) Conveyance and Appurtenances

Inspect spouts, distributors, transitions, and drives for alignment, wear, and dust leakage.

7) Safety Signage and Access Control

Confirm confined‑space/LOTO signage, door/hatch function, and contact info are in place.

Quick Inspection Checklist

  • Document date, conditions, and inspectors
  • Photograph findings with precise locations
  • Prioritize hazards → leaks → developing defects
  • Create work orders and schedule re‑checks
