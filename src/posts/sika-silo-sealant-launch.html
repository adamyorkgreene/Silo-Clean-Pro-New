Global chemicals specialist Sika has introduced Sikaflex‑403 Tank and Silo, a next‑generation, one‑part, moisture‑curing elastic sealant engineered for demanding silo and tank applications. The formulation delivers enhanced durability and outstanding resistance to chemicals, including organic acids.

Purpose‑Built for Tanks and Silos

Developed by Sika’s industry‑leading chemists, Sikaflex‑403 Tank and Silo is suited to segmented or bolted steel tanks and silos—including glass‑fused‑to‑steel, epoxy‑coated, and stainless‑steel systems—as well as wall‑to‑floor connection joints. It can also be used for floor and wall joints in concrete drive‑in silos.

Performance Highlights

  • Enhanced durability, temperature resistance, and movement capability
  • Outstanding resistance to organic acids for agriculture, food, and waste sectors
  • Suitable for domestic and municipal sewage, liquid manure, and silage liquid
  • Withstands temperatures up to 65°C in digestion liquids—ideal for biogas tanks

Tested with Industry Leaders

Sika collaborated with Permastore—a technical leader in modular bolted containment and storage—to trial and test the product ahead of its UK launch. Ensuring leak‑free performance is critical in this sector to avoid environmental contamination and protect waterways.

Why It Matters

Robust sealing is vital for the long‑term performance of tanks and silos. By pairing strong chemical resistance with flexibility and temperature resilience, Sikaflex‑403 helps operators maintain compliance and uptime across agriculture, food processing, and organic waste/biogas applications.
